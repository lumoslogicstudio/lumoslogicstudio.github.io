Under The Stack privacy policy

Last updated: October 12, 2026

This policy applies to the private uploader used by Lumos Logic Studio to manage videos for the Under The Stack YouTube channel.

How the uploader uses YouTube data

The uploader is used only by the channel owner. With the owner’s authorization, it uses the YouTube Data API to upload videos, set video metadata, and verify that it is connected to the intended Under The Stack channel.

Data we collect

We do not collect, sell, or share viewer personal information through the uploader. It does not provide accounts, analytics services, advertising, or tracking for channel viewers.

OAuth authorization data

The channel owner’s OAuth authorization token is stored locally on the owner’s computer and is used only to operate the Under The Stack channel. It is not shared with third parties. The owner can revoke access at any time from their Google Account security settings.

YouTube and Google

Watching videos on YouTube is governed by YouTube’s and Google’s own policies, including Google’s Privacy Policy.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to lumoslogicstudio@gmail.com.
